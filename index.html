What it's all about

Think of it as a small, relaxed cousin of TwitchCon. No convention halls, no lines, no badges. Just friends under one roof with good food, a fire pit and IRL gear rolling.

Every day is packed with easygoing fun, but nothing that wears you out. Nobody has to leave the property all weekend.

When and where

IRL Kickback '27 is a Friday-to-Monday weekend planned for fall 2027. The group will have a private cabin or lodge all to itself somewhere in Indiana, Illinois, Michigan or Wisconsin. Exact dates and location will be announced once the venue is booked.

What to expect at the cabin or lodge

  • One building, so everyone eats, hangs out and IRL streams together
  • A private bedroom for every IRL streamer and their family guests
  • An outdoor fire pit for the nightly gatherings, plus a pool and hot tub if the selected venue has them
  • Golf carts and trails for exploring the property
  • Bag toss, darts and pool for some friendly competition
  • No other groups will be sharing the kitchen, gathering rooms or outdoor spaces
  • A pharmacy and an urgent care or hospital within a short drive, for peace of mind
  • A grocery or big-box store nearby, for anyone who wants to pick up their own snacks, drinks or supplies

Who's coming

It's small on purpose. Spots are reserved first come, first served, with room for 8 to 10 IRL streamers. Each streamer is welcome to bring up to two family guests, as long as everyone fits comfortably in the assigned room.

The group is a mix of IRL streamers from all walks of life, and the pace is relaxed and easygoing. Nobody's rushing anywhere, and everyone is welcome here, just as they are.

Every guest streamer gets their own private bedroom, to share only with their significant other or child. Rooms can't be shared with, or rented out to, anyone else, and only registered attendees may stay overnight.

The weekend

Breakfast, an afternoon of easy games around the property, dinner together, then the fire pit or a large social area until people turn in.

    Evening Social each night means the fire pit or social area, the pool and hot tub if the venue has them, drinks, conversation, streaming and relaxing, for as late as you like.

    What's included and what to bring

    Lodging, meals and golf carts are covered with your stay. A meal prep provider will handle the food, so no one has to cook unless they want to.

    Included

    • A private bedroom for each IRL streamer and their family guests
    • Breakfast every morning
    • Afternoon snack Saturday and Sunday
    • Dinner every night
    • Golf carts, bag toss, dartboards and more

    Bring your own

    • Your IRL streaming setup, plus a Starlink or other backup internet device
    • Alcoholic beverages, if you'd like some in the evenings (alcohol is not provided)
    • Bait and a fishing pole, if you like to fish

    Good to know

    • Age rule: No one under 18 may attend unless a parent or legal guardian is with them for the entire event.
    • Getting there: Guests are responsible for their own transportation to and from the event, including the cost.
    • Attend at your own risk: The IRL Kickback organizers are not responsible for any injury, illness, death, or loss or damage to property, whether at the event or while traveling to and from it.
    • Pets: Most venues don't allow pets, so please leave them at home. Trained service dogs, like guide dogs, are always welcome. Just let us know on your sign-up form. Because of venue rules, we can't make room for emotional support or companion animals.
    • Smoking and vaping: Outside only.
    • Your privacy: The venue needs everyone's legal name, which is shared only with the venue owners. At the event, everyone goes by their streamer name.

    Watch along

    Every IRL streamer will be live during the weekend, so viewers can hop between channels and catch the same moments from different angles: the drive in, the golf cart hunts, the tournaments and the late nights by the fire pit.

    The list of IRL streamers and their channels will be shared closer to the event.

    How to sign up

    Spots go first come, first served. Your spot is held once your form is in, every adult has signed the waiver, and your room payment is received.

    1. Download and print both forms.
      Sign-up form (PDF)Waiver (PDF)
    2. Fill them out and sign. Every adult signs the waiver. A parent or legal guardian signs for anyone under 18.
    3. Email a clear photo or scan of every page. Send them to info@irlkickback.com.
    4. Pay for your room by PayPal, Zelle or Cash App. Room cost and the payment deadline will be announced once the venue is booked.

    Once all spots are filled, new sign-ups go on a waitlist. If a spot opens up, or enough people sign up for a bigger venue, we'll reach out in the order people signed up.
